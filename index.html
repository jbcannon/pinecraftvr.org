Forestry Training Simulator

Pinecraft lets you practice managing mixed-age pine stands in virtual reality — mark trees, walk the stand, and see the forest you're building before you ever step outside.

Why Mixed-Age Forests?

Mixed-age pine management calls for judgment that's hard to teach from a book — you have to see the stand and make the call. Pinecraft gives you a low-stakes place to build that judgment before you're marking trees in the field.

Features

● Load Your Own Stands

Represent pine stands from a CSV — use a built-in stand or bring your own data.

● Mark Trees

Paint trees for marking directly in VR, just like you would in the field.

● View With & Without Marks

Toggle marked trees on and off to compare before and after.

● POV & Bird's-Eye Views

Walk the stand at eye level, or pull up for a bird's-eye view of the whole area.

● Export Marked Trees

Export your marking session to take the results back out of VR.

Rapid Forest Structure View

Quickly visualize stand structure across a whole area.

Harvest Statistics

Stumpage and other harvest statistics, calculated from your marking session.
