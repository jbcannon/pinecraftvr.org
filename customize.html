Advanced Settings

Blank by default, so every stand is different. Enter a number to reproduce the same stand again later.

Defect Rates (%)

A tree marked as a snag is recorded as dead, with every other defect flag forced off. A snag isn't also lopsided or fire-scarred, it's just dead.

Loading Your Stand Into Pinecraft

Drag and drop the downloaded CSV into the SavedData/Local folder inside your Pinecraft install directory, then choose "Load Session" in-game. Pinecraft loads the first CSV alphabetically in that folder, so keep just one stand in there at a time. See the Getting Started guide for the full walkthrough.
