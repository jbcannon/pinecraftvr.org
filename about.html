Ecological Forestry

Ecological forestry is an umbrella term for approaches that manage forests by working with natural processes — fire, natural regeneration, structural complexity — rather than against them. Instead of clearcutting and replanting even-aged blocks, these approaches keep a forest continuously stocked with trees of many ages and sizes, removing individual trees or small groups based on their condition and role in the stand, so the forest is never fully cleared and reset.

One well-documented example is the Stoddard-Neel approach, a system of uneven-aged, single-tree selection forestry developed in the Red Hills region of southwest Georgia and north Florida — the same longleaf pine country where the Jones Center at Ichauway sits. It traces back to Herbert Stoddard, a wildlife biologist and forester whose early-20th- century bobwhite quail research led him to a broader philosophy of land management, later carried forward and refined by his colleague Leon Neel into a full forestry practice built on single-tree marking and frequent prescribed fire.

Ecological forestry is also a skill that takes years to learn — the only way to develop a forester's eye for a good mark has traditionally been to walk stands with an experienced mentor, tree by tree, season after season.

Why We Built Pinecraft

Pinecraft exists to close the gap between reading about single-tree selection and actually practicing it. Marking a stand well means weighing dozens of judgment calls per acre, and in the field, feedback on those calls is slow — you don't really know how a mark turned out until the harvest is done and the stand has had time to respond.

Practice, Repeated

In VR or on PC, Pinecraft lets you mark a stand, see the results instantly, and try again — as many times as it takes to build that judgment. Load a real stand you've measured in the field, or generate one with our Stand Generator, and practice ecological forestry in a low-stakes setting before you're marking trees for real.

Partners

The Jones Center at Ichauway

A private nonprofit research and education organization in southwest Georgia, studying and demonstrating ecological forestry and land management across a working longleaf pine landscape. Pinecraft is a project of the Center's Landscape Ecology Lab.

Devhouse Games

A game development studio that built Pinecraft's simulation in Unreal Engine — the stand rendering, tree marking, and PC/VR gameplay you see in the demo.

USDA NRCS

The USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service supports Pinecraft's development as part of its work helping landowners and land managers put ecological forestry into practice.
